About

The Creative Mind Behind the Stories

Pinisetty Sriramchand — Digital Marketer & Consumer Ads Specialist.

Sriram is a Digital Marketer and Consumer Ads Specialist focused on creating premium, emotionally engaging advertisements for consumer and packaged-goods brands. His key strength is storytelling — transforming everyday products from a simple shelf item into a compelling visual experience that creates craving, comfort and desire.

He specialises in cinematic consumer-product advertisements, seasonal and limited-edition launch campaigns, family and everyday-occasion storytelling, creative direction, AI-powered content creation and digital marketing — with flagship work including campaigns for Gadre Seafood's FishFingers and ProDough's Pumpkin Spice protein range.

His focus is helping consumer brands elevate advertising from routine product promotion to powerful brand storytelling that captures attention, builds emotional connection and turns a quick bite or seasonal treat into something people crave and remember.

Campaigns built to be felt, not just seen.

Four completed consumer campaigns — press play above to watch, or open the case study below for the creative breakdown behind each film.

01
Gadre Seafood — FishFingers

"Air-Fryer Ready" Campaign Film

A direct-to-camera consumer advertisement built around an unscripted-feeling air-fryer demo that ends in a genuine, satisfied bite.

Objective — position Gadre FishFingers as the easiest, tastiest weeknight solution.

The Challenge
Frozen fish fingers are an easy sell but an unglamorous category — most ads default to flat package shots and stock kitchen footage.
Creative Idea
Frame the product as a genuine "I can't believe how good this is" reaction — an air-fryer demo that plays out in real time and ends in a real bite.
Visual Direction
Handheld-feeling kitchen setting, warm cabinet lighting, macro air-fryer basket reveal, natural talent delivery.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted pacing and cut selection to keep the demo-to-bite arc tight within a 20-second format.
Storytelling
Curiosity → demo → crunch → satisfaction → brand, told through one continuous kitchen moment.
Final Advertisement
20-second air-fryer demo film — watch it above.
02
ProDough Whey Protein

"25G Protein?!" Launch Film

A seasonal launch advertisement that opens on an indulgent, swirled pumpkin-spice spoonful and only reveals the protein twist once the craving is already set.

Objective — flip the "protein powder" stereotype and make ProDough Pumpkin Spice feel like dessert, not a supplement.

The Challenge
Protein powders read as clinical and joyless; ProDough needed its Pumpkin Spice flavor to feel like a dessert first, a supplement second.
Creative Idea
Open on an indulgent spoonful and only reveal the "25g protein" twist after the craving is already set up.
Visual Direction
Soft window light, swirled pudding macro shots, warm pumpkin-spice palette, a genuine tasting reaction.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted on-screen text timing and pacing to land the "25G Protein?!" reveal at peak curiosity.
Storytelling
Craving → bite → surprise → reason to believe → brand, using the twist as the hook.
Final Advertisement
20-second seasonal launch film — watch it above.
03
Gadre Seafood — FishFingers

"Crunch Reveal" Ad

A sensory-first consumer advertisement built around one macro bite: hands pulling a fish finger apart to release steam and reveal the flaky fish inside.

Objective — sell the sensory payoff of the bite, not just the plate.

Craving→Crunch→Reveal→Product→Brand
The Challenge
The product's real selling point — a crisp crumb giving way to steaming, flaky fish — never survives a static package shot.
Creative Idea
Build the entire film around one macro bite: hands pulling a fish finger apart to release steam and reveal the fish inside.
Visual Direction
Extreme macro focus, warm kitchen backdrop thrown out of focus, visible steam, unhurried pacing on the reveal.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted shot sequencing to time the steam and pull-apart moment for maximum sensory payoff.
Storytelling
Craving → Crunch → Reveal → Product → Brand — a single sensory payoff instead of a feature list.
Final Advertisement
Macro "crunch reveal" campaign film — watch it above.
04
ProDough Whey Protein

"This Is Protein?!" Campaign

A cozy, testimonial-style advertisement in which a warm on-camera delivery carries the surprise — a bowl of pumpkin-spice pudding presented like a secret worth sharing.

Objective — turn a functional nutrition fact into a cozy, craveable seasonal ritual.

The Challenge
"This is protein?!" needed to land as delighted disbelief, not a hard-sell nutrition claim, to fit ProDough's cozy seasonal branding.
Creative Idea
Let a warm, testimonial-style delivery carry the surprise — presenting the bowl to camera like letting the viewer in on a secret.
Visual Direction
Golden-hour kitchen light, pumpkins and cozy sweaters in frame, a genuine smile-to-camera close.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted caption timing to sync "25G Protein" and "This is protein?!" text beats with the delivery.
Storytelling
Disbelief → proof → comfort → brand — the protein claim becomes a cozy-season ritual, not a stat.
Final Advertisement
20-second testimonial-style campaign film — watch it above.
