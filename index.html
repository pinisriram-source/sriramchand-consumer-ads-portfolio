About

The Creative Mind Behind the Stories

Pinisetty Sriramchand — Digital Marketer & Consumer Ads Specialist.

Sriram is a Digital Marketer and Consumer Ads Specialist focused on creating premium, emotionally engaging advertisements for consumer and packaged-goods brands. His key strength is storytelling — transforming everyday products from a simple shelf item into a compelling visual experience that creates craving, comfort and desire.

His focus is helping consumer brands elevate advertising from routine product promotion to powerful brand storytelling that captures attention, builds emotional connection and turns a quick bite or seasonal treat into something people crave and remember.

01
Viblendi — Protein Smoothies

"The Handoff" Campaign Film

A direct, real-time consumer advertisement that follows a fresh protein smoothie from blender to counter to a genuine customer handoff — three vibrant flavors poured, served and sipped in one continuous shop moment.

Objective — position Viblendi as the neighborhood's go-to fresh-blended protein stop, where real ingredients and real counter service replace shortcuts.

The Challenge
Protein smoothies are usually sold through static product shots or studio pours — the real appeal, a fresh-blended drink handed to you by a real person in a real shop, rarely makes it into the ad.
Creative Idea
Follow one drink's actual journey through the shop: blend, pour, counter handoff, first sip — proving freshness instead of claiming it.
Visual Direction
Bright, sunlit smoothie-bar interior, blender and syrup station visible in the background, natural customer-staff interaction, macro pour and garnish shots across all three flavors — green, mango and mixed berry — each topped with fresh fruit.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted pacing to compress the full blend-to-sip journey into a tight, scroll-stopping format without losing the handoff moment.
Storytelling
Craving → Blend → Handoff → Sip → Brand, told through one uninterrupted in-store moment.
Final Advertisement
20-second in-store reveal film — watch it above.
02
Provana — Whey Protein

"25G Protein?!" Launch Film

A seasonal launch advertisement that opens on an indulgent, swirled pumpkin-spice spoonful and only reveals the protein twist once the craving is already set.

Objective — flip the "protein powder" stereotype and make Provana Pumpkin Spice feel like dessert, not a supplement.

The Challenge
Protein powders read as clinical and joyless; Provana needed its Pumpkin Spice flavor to feel like a dessert first, a supplement second.
Creative Idea
Open on an indulgent spoonful and only reveal the "25G Protein" twist as on-screen text after the craving is already set up.
Visual Direction
Soft window light, swirled pudding macro shots, a warm pumpkin-spice palette with pumpkins in frame, a genuine tasting reaction.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted on-screen text timing and pacing to land the "25G Protein?!" reveal at peak curiosity.
Storytelling
Craving → bite → surprise → reason to believe → brand, using the twist as the hook.
Final Advertisement
20-second seasonal launch film — watch it above.
03
Viblendi — Protein Smoothies

"In Her Words" Testimonial Ad

An on-camera testimonial filmed inside the Viblendi shop — a genuine customer talks through why she chose the protein smoothie lineup, then tastes all three flavors on camera.

Objective — let a real customer's unscripted reaction do the selling, turning product trust into word-of-mouth-style proof.

Trust→Taste→Reaction→Proof→Brand
The Challenge
Protein smoothie claims — real fruit, real protein, no shortcuts — can sound like marketing copy unless a real customer says them first.
Creative Idea
Hand the story to an actual customer: filmed in-store, talking through the flavor lineup and tasting each one on camera, so the proof feels like word of mouth, not a script.
Visual Direction
Natural shop lighting, blender and syrup station visible behind her, all three flavors lined up on the counter, genuine tasting reactions and smiles across each sip.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted trimming and pacing to keep the unscripted testimonial tight and watchable within a 20-second format.
Storytelling
Trust → Taste → Reaction → Proof → Brand — the sell carried by a real voice, not a script.
Final Advertisement
20-second in-store testimonial film — watch it above.
04
Provana — Whey Protein

"This Is Protein?!" Campaign

An extended, elaborately narrated cut of the same launch moment — a warm on-camera testimonial that walks through grass-fed whey, digestion support and muscle recovery before the reveal lands.

Objective — turn functional nutrition facts (grass-fed whey, no added sugar, muscle recovery) into a cozy, craveable seasonal ritual.

The Challenge
"This is protein?!" needed to land as delighted disbelief, not a hard-sell nutrition claim, to fit Provana's cozy seasonal branding — even with a longer, more detailed narration to carry.
Creative Idea
Let a warm, testimonial-style delivery carry the surprise — walking through the pack's grass-fed whey, digestion and muscle-recovery claims like letting the viewer in on a secret, before presenting the bowl to camera.
Visual Direction
Golden-hour kitchen light, pumpkins and a cozy knit sweater in frame, a genuine smile-to-camera close holding the Provana pack.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted caption timing to sync "25G Protein" and "This is protein?!" text beats with the longer narrated delivery.
Storytelling
Disbelief → proof → comfort → brand — the protein claim becomes a cozy-season ritual, not a stat.
Final Advertisement
20-second narrated testimonial-style campaign film — watch it above.
05
Marivo — Tilapia Fish Fingers

"Air-Fryer Ready" Campaign Film

A direct-to-camera consumer advertisement that follows the product from bag to air-fryer basket to a genuine, fork-ready bite at the table.

Objective — position Marivo Tilapia Fish Fingers as the easiest, tastiest weeknight solution, backed by "The Goodness of the Sea."

The Challenge
Frozen fish fingers are an easy sell but an unglamorous category — most ads default to flat package shots and stock kitchen footage.
Creative Idea
Frame the product as a genuine "I can't believe how good this is" reaction — an air-fryer demo that plays out in real time and ends in a real bite.
Visual Direction
Handheld-feeling kitchen setting, warm cabinet lighting, macro air-fryer basket reveal, natural talent delivery holding the Marivo pack to camera.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted pacing and cut selection to keep the bag-to-basket-to-bite arc tight within a 10-second format.
Storytelling
Curiosity → demo → crunch → satisfaction → brand, told through one continuous kitchen moment.
Final Advertisement
10-second air-fryer demo film — watch it above.
06
Marivo — Tilapia Fish Fingers

"Crunch Reveal" Ad

A sensory-first consumer advertisement built around one macro bite — a crisp crumb shattering to reveal flaky tilapia inside, finished with a dip into a herbed tartar sauce.

Objective — sell the sensory payoff of the bite and the dip, not just the plate.

Craving→Crunch→Reveal→Product→Brand
The Challenge
The product's real selling point — a crisp crumb giving way to steaming, flaky tilapia — never survives a static package shot.
Creative Idea
Build the entire film around one macro bite: a fish finger snapped apart to release crumb and steam, then dipped into a herbed tartar sauce.
Visual Direction
Extreme macro focus, softly blurred kitchen backdrop, visible steam and falling crumb, unhurried pacing on the reveal and dip.
AI & Creative Technology
AI-assisted shot sequencing to time the crumb, steam and dip moments for maximum sensory payoff.
Storytelling
Craving → Crunch → Reveal → Product → Brand — a single sensory payoff instead of a feature list.
Final Advertisement
Macro "crunch reveal" campaign film — watch it above.
